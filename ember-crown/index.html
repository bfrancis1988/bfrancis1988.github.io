Ember Crown

A fantasy card battler for mobile

Six factions. Three lanes. One crown. In a fractured realm of monsters, magic, and dying kingdoms, lead your commanders across a three-lane battlefield and outmaneuver rival warlords in tactical asynchronous combat. Build a deck, summon your champions, and seize what's left of the throne.
